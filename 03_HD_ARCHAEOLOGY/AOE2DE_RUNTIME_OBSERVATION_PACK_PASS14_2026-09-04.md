## 4. Critical evidence rule

An `ACTION` event is a **command observation**, not automatically a completed world-state observation.

Therefore:

`RESEARCH` ≠ research completion  
`BUILD` ≠ completed building  
`DE_QUEUE` ≠ completed unit stock  
`DE_ATTACK_MOVE` ≠ successful attack  
`MOVE` ≠ arrival  
`SELL/BUY` ≠ proof of subsequent strategic benefit  
`RESIGN` = terminal player decision, but not a causal explanation of preceding state.

This preserves the Pass-13 W0→W4 hierarchy.

## 15. Historical boundary

Historical claims remain grounded in verified `.per` source. Replay evidence can promote a world-state relationship only when the observed event and resulting state can be causally joined and provenance is established.
