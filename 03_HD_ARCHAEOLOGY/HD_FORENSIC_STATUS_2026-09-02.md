# HD Forensic Archaeology Status — 2026-09-02

## Current determination

The project has moved from **source preservation** to **forensic reconstruction**.
The recovered HD artifact is now being treated as a strategic-control fossil,
not merely as an old AI script.

## What has been established

- The artifact explicitly attributes itself to the official HD/2013 Edition AI
  created by Promiskuitiv and Archon.
- The source contains thousands of local heuristics organized into named
  strategic sections.
- Goals, strategic numbers, timers, facts, and action rules form a distributed
  state/control substrate.
- Enemy observations can become persistent classifications consumed by later
  controllers.
- Strategy selection can alter military choice, economic allocation, position
  behavior, and timers together.
- Resource-control behavior provides evidence of explicit reservation/opportunity
  cost management.
- Attack and retreat behavior is a stateful feedback controller with distinct
  threat-specific responses and temporal guards.
- Map and position classifications influence strategic decisions upstream.

## What is not yet established

We have not yet reconstructed every writer/reader relationship, every state
transition, every historical branch, or every causal rationale. We therefore do
not claim complete understanding of the designer's mind.

We also do not equate source comments with proof of author intent beyond the
literal semantic contract they state.

## Next forensic objective

Construct the complete **writer-reader-transition graph** for strategically
meaningful goals, strategic numbers, and timers. This graph will identify:

- state ownership;
- competing writers;
- consumers;
- initialization;
- transition predicates;
- action bundles;
- clearing/reset paths;
- temporal guards;
- cross-controller feedback;
- unreachable or obsolete states;
- contradictions and compensating mechanisms.

After that graph, reconstruct the explicit strategy-selection state machine,
then infer implicit principles, then meta-knowledge, then general AoE2 laws.

## Preservation rule

No historical mechanism is discarded merely because it is ugly, duplicated,
obsolete, or architecturally inferior. Such material may be evidence of the
problems the original designers encountered. It is labeled rather than erased.
