# Pass 52 Hostile QC — HD Resource / Escrow / Production / Sustainment Archaeology

**Date:** 2026-09-04  
**Layer:** 2  
**Verdict:** PASS

## QC objective

Attempt to falsify or overstate the Pass-52 conclusion that historical HD AI scripting provides explicit resource-protection / escrow machinery that can participate in purpose-directed capability commitments, while rejecting the stronger unsupported claim that the historical AI was a universal economic optimizer.

## Tests

### 1. Escrow semantics inflated into budgeting
**Result:** PASS.

The pass explicitly distinguishes escrow, budgeting, and optimization. Escrow is treated as protected purchasing power, not as proof of a global budget allocator.

### 2. Escrow treated as free resources
**Result:** PASS.

The analysis preserves the distinction between ordinary affordability and escrow-inclusive affordability. Protected resources are not treated as ordinary free stockpile.

### 3. Release treated as completion
**Result:** PASS.

Release is classified as an authorization/control event. It does not prove research, training, construction, or strategic success.

### 4. `can-X-with-escrow` treated as execution
**Result:** PASS.

The pass distinguishes affordability predicates from side-effecting commands.

### 5. Training command treated as unit completion
**Result:** PASS.

The analysis preserves the replay/archaeology invariant that queue/action evidence is not automatically promoted to completed or surviving capability.

### 6. Historical AI called a universal optimizer
**Result:** PASS.

The conclusion explicitly rejects that claim. Evidence supports explicit controllers and selected commitment paths, not universal marginal-utility calculation.

### 7. Opportunity cost overclaimed as explicit historical computation
**Result:** PASS.

Opportunity cost is described as an economic consequence and an AEGIS interpretation of what resource protection accomplishes, not as a proven historical utility calculation.

### 8. Byzantine Cataphract discount contamination
**Result:** PASS.

The pass explicitly states that the Byzantine -25% discount does not apply to Cataphracts.

### 9. Cataphract affordability confused with sustainment
**Result:** PASS.

Production throughput, resource flow, army mass, reinforcement, and survival are treated as separate constraints.

### 10. Elite and Logistica collapsed into one decision
**Result:** PASS.

Base production, Elite transition, and Logistica are treated as separate capital decisions.

### 11. Imperial timing omitted
**Result:** PASS.

The pass explicitly models Imperial Age as a competing intertemporal investment and preserves the invariant that cheaper Imperial does not guarantee faster Imperial.

### 12. Historical current-version contamination
**Result:** PASS.

Current public references are used only where explicitly identified as current/reference data. They are not presented as installed `.dat` authority.

### 13. HD/UP/DE semantic drift
**Result:** PASS WITH BOUNDARY.

The public encyclopedia notes common core AI-engine applicability across versions. The pass uses command semantics cautiously and does not claim that every exact historical file behavior is identical to current DE behavior.

### 14. Resource-control state semantics invented
**Result:** PASS.

The pass describes `sn-resource-control` as a state/control channel and avoids assigning unsupported universal meanings to every numeric value.

### 15. Emergency cancellation generalized too strongly
**Result:** PASS WITH BOUNDARY.

The pass labels release/reset under changing context as a historical pattern where evidenced, and explicitly leaves exact cross-controller coordination open.

### 16. Production queue interpreted as capacity without qualification
**Result:** PASS.

Queue capacity is treated as a production constraint. The pass does not claim a single universal throughput formula.

### 17. Strategic capital concept mistaken for historical variable
**Result:** PASS.

“Capability Funding State,” “Gold Portfolio Pressure,” and “Strategic Capital Lifecycle” are explicitly marked AEGIS-generalizations.

### 18. Layer-2 boundary violation
**Result:** PASS.

No `.per` implementation, runtime controller, production policy, or deployment artifact was created.

## Final QC verdict

**PASS.**

The research is safe to carry forward as Layer-2 archaeology. The strongest defensible claim is that historical HD AI scripting provides a concrete mechanism for **resource protection and purpose-directed release**, and that this mechanism can participate in explicit capability-commitment chains. The evidence does **not** establish a universal economic optimizer, probabilistic portfolio model, or human-equivalent opportunity-cost calculator.

## Required preservation rules

1. Keep escrow semantics separate from general budgeting.
2. Keep authorization separate from execution and completion.
3. Keep completion separate from sustainment and battlefield outcome.
4. Treat Cataphract as late-game Gold-intensive strategic capital.
5. Keep Byzantine discounted families separate from Cataphract economics.
6. Treat Imperial timing as an intertemporal competitor.
7. Preserve evidence classes DIRECT / COMPOSED / INFERRED / AEGIS-GENERALIZATION / UNCERTAIN.
8. Do not convert any Pass-52 research construct into Layer-3 code before the Layer-2 research boundary is formally closed.
