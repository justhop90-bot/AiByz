## Mission

Force Composition is a conditional semantic layer that preserves materially consequential relationships among multiple military capabilities when those relationships would otherwise be lost through independent capability requirements.

It evaluates qualified contribution relative to an authoritative requirement and publishes sufficient, partial, deficient, or unknown composition state without selecting realizations, courses, allocations, commitments, commands, or strategic outcomes.

## Primary boundary

```text
CAPABILITY REQUIREMENT
        ↓
QUALIFIED EXISTING CONTRIBUTION
        ↓
FUNCTIONAL COMPOSITION
        ↓
MATERIAL MAGNITUDE / RELATIONSHIPS
        ↓
SUFFICIENT / PARTIAL / DEFICIENT / UNKNOWN
        ↓
CAPABILITY FACTORY / PLANNING
```

Force Composition exists only where the relationship among multiple capabilities creates behavioral information that independent capability requirements would lose.

A single-component requirement should collapse to Capability Factory when composition adds no independent behavioral return.
