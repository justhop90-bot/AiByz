# AEGIS Recovery — Five-Pass Closure

Date: 2026-09-05
Status: CLOSED — ARCHITECTURE

## Mission
Convert qualified Verification results into a bounded, identity-safe response to the current course without exceeding the evidence or taking ownership of upstream strategic systems.

## Five-pass result
1. Architect — PASS
2. Carpenter — PASS
3. Adversary — PASS WITH TARGETED CORRECTIONS
4. Scientist — PASS WITH OPEN EMPIRICAL QUESTIONS
5. Systems Assurance — PASS; subsystem closed for architecture

## Contract
Recovery owns current-course viability assessment, response disposition, bounded retry/repetition, material-change sensitivity, recovery identity/generation linkage, consequential information dependencies, upstream reassessment requests, and recovery-result publication.

Recovery does not own World Model, Observation, Belief, Situation, Objectives, Planning, Decision, Commitment, Resources, Capability, Production, Commands, Execution, Verification, Attention, Scheduler, Memory, Risk, Doctrine, or Opponent Model.

## Load-bearing invariants
- Verification evidence drives consequential Recovery.
- Identity and generation survive Verification → Recovery.
- Stale results cannot control current Recovery.
- UNKNOWN remains UNKNOWN; partial remains partial.
- Missing evidence is not automatic failure.
- Cancellation does not erase world effects; supersession changes authority, not history.
- Operational success/failure does not equal strategic Objective success/failure.
- Recovery cannot invent plans, decisions, commitments, commands, schedules, or world facts.
- Retry and repeated response are bounded; equivalent failure cannot create unlimited repetition.
- Information dependencies flow through Attention/Scheduler; replacement feasibility through Planning; replacement selection through Decision; responsibility through Commitment; operational action through Execution.
- Machine representation remains target-build and ABI qualified.

## Closure
Architecture is closed. ABI allocation, runtime implementation, and production `.per` are explicitly deferred to later qualification gates.
