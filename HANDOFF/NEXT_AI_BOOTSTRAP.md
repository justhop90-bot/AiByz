## First day
Verify the repository branches and current authoritative files. Locate the PC project and replay roots. Hash critical raw artifacts. Confirm AoE2DE executable version/hash. Confirm Ghidra installation and whether an analysis project/log remains.

## First research session
Do not write the bot. Reproduce one calibration extraction and one replay parse. Confirm ACTION envelope and DE_QUEUE schema. Re-run temporal checks. Confirm which rich SYNC records are available.

## First empirical objective
Complete Production-to-Object Lineage Reconstruction using raw calibration streams. Quantify:
- queue command count;
- amount/commitment magnitude;
- producer identity continuity;
- queue intervals;
- object birth candidates;
- completion latency candidates;
- false lineage links;
- unresolved production events;
- cancellation/interruption signatures;
- capability availability latency.

## Research hygiene
Every result must declare its evidence class. Never promote a probable inference to confirmed without an independent falsification-resistant test.

## Architectural destination
The eventual bot should implement explicit state, uncertainty, objectives, capability models, opponent beliefs, production authority, execution acknowledgment, and learning—not a direct transplant of V3 or experimental architectures.

## Strategic destination
Build a controller capable of selecting actions based on state + uncertainty + objective + available actions, with expected consequence, risk, failure signature, recovery, reversibility, and commitment magnitude.

## Byzantine destination
Optimize for conversion of enemy commitments into disproportionate strategic burden. Measure the burden empirically before encoding it as doctrine.

## Final warning
Do not trust the conversation as the source of truth. The conversation is a research trail. The repository, raw artifacts, hashes, evidence ledgers, and authority rules are the durable system.
