## Core conceptual discoveries

1. Strategy is controlled state transformation under uncertainty and resource constraints.
2. Capability is more informative than raw unit count.
3. Resources have state-dependent opportunity cost.
4. Production is conversion of resources/infrastructure into future capability.
5. Position modifies capability value.
6. Timing creates capability windows.
7. Initiative is control over the opponent's decision set.
8. Commitments consume optionality and should have reversibility classes.
9. Opponent modeling should predict transitions, not merely classify current composition.
10. Counters should target capabilities and transitions, not only units.
11. Information has acquisition cost and decision value.
12. Failure should update beliefs and trigger scoped recovery.
13. Strategic value must be compared against feasible alternatives.
14. Strategic dimensions interact; a purely additive utility model is insufficient.
15. Robustness matters in addition to expected value.
16. Context changes policy priors without changing the underlying ontology.

## Evidence discipline

Historical source is evidence for what designers attempted and for recurring strategic structures. It is not automatically proof that every heuristic is optimal.

Independent machine evidence establishes engine mechanics.

Replay evidence establishes empirical game outcomes.

AEGIS policy is derived only after evidence and reasoning are separated.
