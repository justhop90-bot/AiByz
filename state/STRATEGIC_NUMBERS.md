## Rule

Strategic numbers are a distinct state channel from goals. A numeric value observed in stock or Promisory does not become an AEGIS SN merely because it is unused elsewhere.

Every allocation requires owner, semantic type, read/write behavior, lifecycle, stock collision review, and target-build qualification.
